How to use it

  1. Tap Choose a small photo, for example an old picture, a logo or a screenshot.
  2. Wait while the AI works piece by piece. A progress bar shows how far it is.
  3. Drag across the picture to compare before and after, then tap Download.

Which devices work?

  • Best: computers with a recent Chrome or Edge (they have graphics speed-up). Pictures up to about 1000 pixels wide take about 1–3 minutes.
  • Other devices (Firefox, older Safari, most phones): works, but much slower, so the picture is first made small (up to 384 pixels).
  • This is the heaviest AI tool on the site. Your device may get warm. That is normal and safe.

Common questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI runs inside your browser. Your photo never leaves your device and we do not save anything.

Can this harm my device or let someone hack it?

No. It runs inside the browser's safety box like any web page and can only see the one photo you pick. The program files come from this website and from jsDelivr, a large open-source code site, locked to exact versions. The AI model comes from Hugging Face, locked to one exact version that cannot be changed. Closing the tab stops everything.

Can it add details that were never there?

No AI can truly recover lost details. It makes edges sharper and smoother, which looks much better than normal stretching. Faces and small text in very blurry photos may still look soft.

Which AI does it use?

Swin2SR (2× classical super-resolution), an open-source model with the Apache 2.0 licence, running with Transformers.js.
