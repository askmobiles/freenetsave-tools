Common questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Each Word?

Title Case keeps small words like "and", "of" and "the" in lower case, the way book and article titles are written. Capitalize Each Word makes every word start with a capital.

What is Sentence case?

Only the first letter of each sentence is a capital, like normal writing. Names will need to be fixed by hand afterwards.

What are camelCase and snake_case for?

They are ways of joining words without spaces, used in computer code, file names and spreadsheets.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser and is gone when you close the page.
