Yearly thithi (remembrance day) · வருட திதி

Enter the date a loved one passed away. You get the Tamil month and thithi of that day, and the date it falls on this year.
இறந்த தேதியை இடுங்கள். அந்த நாளின் தமிழ் மாதம், திதி மற்றும் இந்த ஆண்டு திதி வரும் தேதி கிடைக்கும்.

The yearly thithi is found the Tamil way: the same Tamil month and the same thithi, on the day that thithi covers the afternoon. It uses the method (Thirukanitham or Vakyam) and the place chosen on this page. Without a time, the thithi at sunrise on the day of passing is used. Families and temples follow slightly different rules, so please confirm the date with your priest.
ஒரே தமிழ் மாதம், ஒரே திதி என்ற முறையில் கணிக்கப்படுகிறது. தேதியை உங்கள் குருக்களிடம் உறுதி செய்யுங்கள்.

About this page

The Malayalam calendar of Kerala is a solar calendar, like the Tamil one. Its year, counted in the Kollam era, begins on 1 Chingam in mid-August. Dates here are calculated and can differ by a day from a printed Kerala calendar near the start of a month.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

When does the Malayalam year begin?

On 1 Chingam, in mid-August.

When is Vishu?

On 1 Medam, on 14 or 15 April.
