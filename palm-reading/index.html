Your palm reading

Palm reading is a very old tradition from India, China and Europe. It is for fun and reflection only. It cannot predict the future or tell anything about health.

The main lines and mounts

  • Heart line: love and feelings
  • Head line: thinking style
  • Life line: energy and big changes (not how long you live)
  • Fate line: career and life path (not everyone has one)
  • Mounts: the soft pads under each finger, at the base of the thumb and on the outer edge

Common questions

How do you read your palm?

Start with your hand shape, then look at the four main lines: the heart line (top), head line (middle), life line (around the thumb) and fate line (up the middle). Then check which soft pad (mount) is the fullest. This tool walks you through each one with pictures.

Which hand should I read?

Most palm readers use the hand you write with for your present and future, and the other hand for the traits you were born with. You can do the reading twice and compare.

Does the life line show how long I will live?

No. That is a common myth. In palm reading the life line is about energy and big life changes, not the length of your life.

What if I do not have a fate line?

That is normal. Many people do not have a clear fate line. Traditionally it means you like freedom and make your own path.

Do you save my palm photo?

No. The photo is shown only on your own screen. It is never uploaded or saved, and it is gone when you close the page.

Is palm reading real?

Palm reading (palmistry, or kai regai in Tamil) is a very old tradition, but there is no scientific proof that it predicts the future. Enjoy it for fun and reflection.
