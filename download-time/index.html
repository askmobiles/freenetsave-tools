Common questions

What is the difference between MB and Mb?

File sizes use megabytes (MB). Internet speeds use megabits per second (Mbps). One byte is 8 bits, so a 100 Mbps connection downloads about 12.5 MB per second.

Why is my download slower?

Wi-Fi distance, busy times, other devices and the website's own server all slow things down. Real downloads are often 60–90% of the plan speed.

Does it work for uploads?

Yes. Type your upload speed instead. Upload speeds on many home plans are much lower than download speeds.
