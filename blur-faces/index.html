How to use it

  1. Choose how to hide the faces: blur, pixels, a black box or a smiley.
  2. Tap Choose a photo. The AI finds the faces and hides them.
  3. Tap any missed face to hide it too, or tap a hidden face to show it again. Then tap Download.

Which devices work?

  • Almost every computer, phone and tablet with a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. This is the lightest of our AI tools.
  • It needs graphics support (WebGL), which nearly every browser has.

Common questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The face finder runs inside your browser. Your photo never leaves your device and we do not save anything.

Can this harm my device or let someone hack it?

No. It runs inside the browser's safety box like any web page and can only see the one photo you pick. All program and AI files come from this website. Closing the tab stops everything.

Is the blur safe to share?

Yes. The downloaded picture is a new image with the faces covered. The original faces are not hidden inside it. For the strongest privacy, choose Black box.

Does it recognise who people are?

No. It only finds where faces are. It does not know or remember who anyone is.

Which AI does it use?

Google MediaPipe Face Detection (Apache 2.0 licence), hosted on this website.
