How to sign a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Draw your signature with your finger or mouse, or type your name.
  3. Tap the page where it should go. Drag it to move it, and use the slider to change the size.
  4. Tap Save signed PDF.

This puts a picture of your signature on the page, like signing with a pen. It is not a digital certificate. Ask the person who needs the form if a drawn signature is accepted.

🔒 Your PDF is handled inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
