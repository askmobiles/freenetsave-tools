About this page

The Ethiopian calendar has 13 months: twelve of 30 days and a short thirteenth month of 5 or 6 days. Its year is seven to eight years behind the English calendar, and the New Year, Enkutatash, is in September.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

Why is the Ethiopian year different?

It counts the years from a different date for the birth of Christ, so it is 7 or 8 years behind.

When is Ethiopian New Year?

On 11 September, or 12 September before a leap year.
