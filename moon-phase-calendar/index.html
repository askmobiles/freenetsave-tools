About this page

This page works out the Moon's phase from its real position. The month grid shows how much of the Moon is lit each day and marks the new moon, the quarters and the full moon. The full moon names, such as Wolf Moon and Harvest Moon, are the traditional North American ones used in farmers' almanacs.

Planting by the Moon is an old custom: leafy crops while the Moon grows, root crops while it shrinks. It is shown here as a tradition, not as tested advice.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

When is the next full moon?

The date is shown in the details, in world (UTC) time. In your own time zone it can fall on the day before or after.

Why does each full moon have a name?

Farmers and First Nations in North America named the moons after the season, such as the Strawberry Moon in June.

Can I print a moon calendar?

Yes. Press Print this month under the moon phase calendar.
