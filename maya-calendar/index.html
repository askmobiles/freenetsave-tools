About this page

The Maya kept several counts at once. The Tzolk'in is a sacred count of 260 days, made of 13 numbers and 20 day signs. The Haab' is a 365-day year of 18 months of 20 days plus 5 extra days. The Long Count counts every day from a starting date in 3114 BC. Day-keepers in the highlands of Guatemala still follow the Tzolk'in to choose days for ceremonies.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

What is my Maya day sign?

Enter your birth date. The Tzolk'in line shows the number and the day sign for that day.

Did the Maya calendar end in 2012?

No. A large cycle of the Long Count was completed on 21 December 2012 and a new one began. The count simply continues.

Which correlation does this use?

The standard GMT correlation, number 584283, used by most researchers.
