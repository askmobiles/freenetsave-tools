Dial *#06# on the phone. Or look in Settings → About phone (Android) or Settings → General → About (iPhone). It's also printed on the box and the SIM tray on many phones.

⚠️ This checker only tells you if the number is correctly formed. It cannot tell you if a phone is stolen, blocked, locked to a carrier or under warranty — ask your carrier for that.
