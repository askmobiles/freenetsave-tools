Online Notepad

Write notes in your browser. They stay on this device only.

🔒 Notes are kept only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste in the box. Your note is kept in this browser on this device as you type.
  2. Tap New note to start another one. Pick a note from the list to switch.
  3. Tap Download as .txt to keep a copy as a file, or Open a .txt file to edit one you have.

Common questions

Where are my notes saved?

Only in this browser on this device. They are never sent to us or to anyone. If you clear your browser data, use another browser or use a private window, the notes will not be there, so download anything important.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Can I open my notes on another device?

Not automatically, because nothing is stored online. Download the note as a .txt file and open it on the other device.

Is there a limit?

Browsers usually allow a few megabytes, which is millions of characters of text.
