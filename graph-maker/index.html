Graph maker

Plot points and lines on a coordinate grid, and see the coordinates of any spot.

Points

One point on each line, as x, y. You can put a name first, like A 2, 3.

Lines and curves

Type a rule with x, like y = 2x + 1 or y = x^2 - 4.

Grid size

Move over the grid, or tap it, to see the coordinates. Tap near a point to read it.

🔒 This tool runs on your device. Nothing you enter is sent or saved.
