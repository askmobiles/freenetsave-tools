This browser cannot record the screen. Please use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer. Phones and tablets do not allow websites to record the screen; use the phone's built-in screen recorder instead.

🔒 Your recording stays on your device. Nothing is sent or saved by us.

How to use it

  1. Choose whether to record your microphone and the sound from the screen.
  2. Tap Start recording. Your browser asks what to share: the whole screen, one window or one tab. Pick one and tap Share.
  3. Tap Stop (or the browser's "Stop sharing" button) when you are done. Watch the video, then tap Save video.

Common questions

Is there a time limit or watermark?

No watermark and no time limit set by us. Very long recordings use a lot of memory, so save long videos in parts.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The recording is made and kept in your browser on your device until you save it.

Why is there no sound from the screen?

Sound can be recorded from a browser tab in Chrome and Edge (tick "Share tab audio"), and from the whole screen on Windows. Mac and Firefox often do not allow screen sound; your microphone still works.

What format is the video?

Newer Chrome and Edge save MP4. Other browsers save WebM, which plays in Chrome, Firefox and VLC. A free converter can change WebM to MP4 if needed.

Does it work on a phone?

No. Phones and tablets do not let websites record the screen. iPhone and Android both have a screen recorder built in, in the quick settings or Control Centre.
