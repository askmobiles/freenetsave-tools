How to add a watermark to a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Type the words, then pick the layout, colour, size and strength. The picture shows page 1.
  3. Tap Save PDF with watermark. The words go on every page.

You can type in any language, including Tamil and Hindi. A watermark shows that a page is a copy or a draft; it does not lock the PDF.

🔒 Your PDF is handled inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
