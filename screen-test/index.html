Screen Test

Check for dead pixels and test the touch screen. Handy when buying a used phone.

Dead pixel test

The screen fills with one colour. Look for tiny dots that stay black, white or the wrong colour. Tap to go to the next colour.

Touch test

Slide your finger over every square until they all turn green. A square that will not turn green means that part of the screen does not sense touch.

🔒 This tool runs on your device. Nothing you enter is sent or saved.
