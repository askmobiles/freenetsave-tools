How to use it

  1. Type your card balance and the yearly interest rate (on your statement, often 19.99% or 22.99% in Canada).
  2. Type what you pay each month to see how long it takes, or switch to Payment to be free by… and choose the number of months.
  3. If you keep using the card, add your new spending each month for a true answer.

Common questions

Why does paying only the minimum take so long?

Most of a small payment goes to interest, so the balance hardly goes down. Paying even a little more each month cuts the time and the interest a lot.

Is this exact?

It is a close estimate. Banks work out interest daily and may change your rate or minimum payment, so your statement can be slightly different.

What if I have more than one card?

Pay the minimum on all cards and put any extra money on the card with the highest interest rate first.
