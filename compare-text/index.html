How to use it

  1. Paste the first (old) text on the left and the second (new) text on the right.
  2. Tap Compare.
  3. Red lines were removed and green lines were added. Inside a changed line, the exact words that changed are highlighted.

Common questions

How does it decide what changed?

It lines up the two texts line by line, finds the longest set of lines they share, and marks everything else as added or removed. Inside a changed line, it does the same with words.

Can I ignore small differences?

Yes. Tick "Ignore extra spaces" or "Ignore upper and lower case".

Is there a size limit?

It works well for documents up to a few thousand lines. For very long files, compare them in parts.

Are my texts uploaded?

No. The comparison happens in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.
