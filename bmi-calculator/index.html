Common questions

What is BMI?

Body mass index is your weight divided by your height squared. It is a quick screening number used for adults.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It does not tell muscle from fat, and it can read high for very muscular people and differ by age and background. Talk to your doctor about your own health.

Can I use it for children?

No. Children and teens under 18 use growth charts for their age and sex. Ask your doctor or nurse.
