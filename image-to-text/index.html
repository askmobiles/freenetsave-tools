How to use it

  1. Choose the language of the text. If the picture mixes two languages, pick the second one too.
  2. Tap Choose a picture, drop a file on the box, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.
  3. Wait a few seconds while the text is read. Then fix any mistakes, copy it or download it.

Common questions

Is my picture uploaded?

No. The text is read by a program that runs inside your browser. The picture never leaves your device.

Why is the first time slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the reader and the language (about 5 to 8 MB). It is kept in your browser, so the next time is much faster.

How can I get better results?

Use a sharp, well-lit picture taken straight on, with dark text on a light background. Crop out anything that is not text. Printed text works much better than handwriting.

Can it read Tamil?

Yes. Choose Tamil as the language. Printed Tamil works well; handwriting is hard for any reader.

Does it work with PDFs?

This page reads pictures. For a PDF that already has text in it, use PDF to text. For a scanned PDF, save the page as a picture first with PDF to JPG.
