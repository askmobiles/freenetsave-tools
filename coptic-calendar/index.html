About this page

The Coptic calendar is the church calendar of Egypt. It has 13 months: twelve of 30 days and a short month of 5 or 6 days, and its years are counted from 284 AD. It sets the feast days and the many fasting days of the Coptic Orthodox Church.

The fasting information here covers the main fasts. The Church keeps other fasts too, so follow your parish calendar.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

When is Coptic Christmas?

On 29 Koiak, which is 7 January.

When is Coptic Easter?

On the Orthodox date, which is often later than Western Easter. The next date is shown in the details.

Which days are fasting days?

Most Wednesdays and Fridays, Great Lent, the Nativity fast and the fast of the Virgin Mary, among others.
