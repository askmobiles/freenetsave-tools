TTC Trip Finder

Scheduled subway, streetcar and bus trips in Toronto, with changes and short walks. Unofficial.

Type a station, stop or street corner and choose it from the list. Leave "To" empty to see the next departures from one stop.

Scheduled times only. This page does not know about delays, diversions, closures or shuttle buses. Walking times are estimates. Check TTC service updates before you travel. This page is not made, approved or endorsed by the TTC.
