How to use it

  1. Paste your list in the top box, one item on each line.
  2. Choose the options you want, for example ignore upper and lower case.
  3. Copy the result or download it as a text file.

Common questions

Which copy is kept?

The first time a line appears is kept, in its original place. Later copies are removed.

What does "ignore upper and lower case" do?

It treats "Apple", "APPLE" and "apple" as the same line, and keeps the first one.

Can I find which lines were repeated?

Yes. Tick "Show only the lines that were repeated" to see just those lines, once each.

Is my list sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
