Common questions

What is an IP address?

It is the number the internet uses to send data to your home or phone network, like a street address for your connection.

Why is the location wrong?

The location belongs to your internet provider's equipment, which can be in another town. It never shows your home address.

Does it change?

Most home and mobile IP addresses change from time to time, for example when the modem restarts. A VPN also changes it.
