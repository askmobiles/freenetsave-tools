How to use it

  1. Choose one or more pictures.
  2. Type your watermark text, for example your name, shop or "© 2026". Pick where it goes, its size, colour and how see-through it is.
  3. Tap Download to save the pictures with the watermark.

Common questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The watermark is drawn by your own browser on your device. Your pictures never leave it.

Which setting stops copying best?

Repeat across the picture is hardest to crop out. A single small mark in a corner looks neater but is easy to cut off.

Does it lower the quality?

The picture keeps its full size. JPG pictures are saved at high quality (92%).
