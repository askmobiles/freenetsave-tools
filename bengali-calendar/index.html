Yearly thithi (remembrance day) · வருட திதி

Enter the date a loved one passed away. You get the Tamil month and thithi of that day, and the date it falls on this year.
இறந்த தேதியை இடுங்கள். அந்த நாளின் தமிழ் மாதம், திதி மற்றும் இந்த ஆண்டு திதி வரும் தேதி கிடைக்கும்.

If you already know the thithi or the Tamil month of passing, choose them here. They are used in place of the ones worked out from the date. With both chosen, you can leave the date empty.

The yearly thithi is found the Tamil way: the same Tamil month and the same thithi, on the day that thithi covers the afternoon. The thithi of passing is worked out for the place of passing. This year's date is worked out for the place chosen at the top of the page, which should be where you will keep the day, using the method (Thirukanitham or Vakyam) chosen there. Without a time, the thithi at sunrise on the day of passing is used. Families and temples follow slightly different rules, so please confirm the date with your priest.
ஒரே தமிழ் மாதம், ஒரே திதி என்ற முறையில் கணிக்கப்படுகிறது. தேதியை உங்கள் குருக்களிடம் உறுதி செய்யுங்கள்.

About this page

This page uses the national Bengali calendar of Bangladesh, where the year always begins on 14 April. West Bengal follows an older astronomical version, so dates there can be a day different and the New Year is often on 15 April.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

When is Pohela Boishakh?

On 14 April in Bangladesh. In West Bengal it is usually 15 April.

How many seasons does the Bengali year have?

Six, each two months long.
