How to use it

  1. Choose the language that is spoken.
  2. Tap Start recording and speak, then tap Stop. Or choose an audio or video file.
  3. Wait while the AI listens. Check the text, then copy or download it. You can also download subtitles for a video.

Which devices work?

  • Computers (Windows, Mac, Chromebook) with a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari work best.
  • Phones and tablets: newer iPhones and Android phones work, but long recordings are slow. Keep it under 2–3 minutes on a phone.
  • Recording needs your permission to use the microphone. The sound stays on your device.

Common questions

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The Whisper AI runs inside your browser. Your audio never leaves your device and we do not save anything.

Can this harm my device or let someone hack it?

No. It runs inside the browser's safety box like any web page. It can only hear the microphone while you are recording, and only after you allow it. The program files come from this website and from jsDelivr, a large open-source code site, locked to exact versions. The AI model comes from Hugging Face, locked to one exact version that cannot be changed. Closing the tab stops everything.

Which languages work?

English works best. French, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese and Tamil also work, but less accurately, especially Tamil. Always read the text and fix mistakes.

How long can the audio be?

Any length, but long audio takes time on your device. On a computer, one minute of speech usually takes under a minute. Under 10 minutes works best.

Which AI does it use?

Whisper base, made open source by OpenAI (MIT licence), running with Transformers.js.
