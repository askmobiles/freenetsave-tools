How to use it

  1. Use a photo of one or more people. It works best when the person stands out from the background.
  2. Choose the new background: clear, white or any colour.
  3. Tap Choose a photo. Wait while the AI works, then tap Download.

Which devices work?

  • Computers (Windows, Mac, Chromebook) with a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari work best.
  • Phones and tablets: newer iPhones and Android phones work. Older phones may be slow.
  • A normal photo takes about 2–10 seconds on a computer, a little longer on a phone.

Common questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI runs inside your browser. Your photo never leaves your device and we do not save anything.

Can this harm my device or let someone hack it?

No. It runs inside the browser's safety box like any web page and can only see the one photo you pick. The program files come from this website and from jsDelivr, a large open-source code site, locked to exact versions. The AI model comes from Hugging Face, locked to one exact version that cannot be changed. At worst your device gets warm or slow for a moment. Closing the tab stops everything.

Why is the first time slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI. It is kept in your browser, so the next time starts much faster.

Which AI does it use?

MODNet, an open-source AI made for cutting out people (Apache 2.0 licence), running with Transformers.js.

The edges are not perfect. What can I do?

Use a photo with good light where the person stands out from the background. This tool is made for people; products and objects are coming later.
