How to use it

  1. Choose Anything for products, food, pets and things, or People and portraits for photos of people.
  2. Tap Choose a photo and wait a few seconds.
  3. Fix any spots: Erase paints away leftover background, Restore paints back parts the AI removed by mistake. Undo takes back the last stroke.
  4. Choose the new background (clear, white or any colour) and tap Download.

Which devices work?

  • Computers (Windows, Mac, Chromebook) with a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari work best.
  • Phones and tablets: newer iPhones and Android phones work. Older phones may be slow.
  • A normal photo takes about 3–15 seconds on a computer, a little longer on a phone.
  • The clean-up brush works with a mouse, a finger or a pen.

Common questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI runs inside your browser. Your photo never leaves your device and we do not save anything.

Can this harm my device or let someone hack it?

No. It runs inside the browser's safety box like any web page and can only see the one photo you pick. The program files come from this website and from jsDelivr, a large open-source code site, locked to exact versions. The AI model comes from Hugging Face, locked to one exact version that cannot be changed. At worst your device gets warm or slow for a moment. Closing the tab stops everything.

Why is the first time slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI. It is kept in your browser, so the next time starts much faster.

Which AI does it use?

For Anything: ORMBG, an open-source background remover (Apache 2.0 licence). For People: MODNet (Apache 2.0 licence). Both run with Transformers.js on your device.

The edges are not perfect. What can I do?

Use the clean-up brush: Erase removes leftover background and Restore brings back parts that were cut off. A photo with good light, where the subject stands out from the background, gives the best result. Pictures with a lot of text or many objects are hard for any AI.
