About this page

The Solar Hijri calendar is the official calendar of Iran and Afghanistan. The year begins at Nowruz, on the spring equinox. The first six months have 31 days, the next five have 30, and the last has 29 or 30.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

When is Nowruz?

On 1 Farvardin, at the spring equinox, on or near 20 or 21 March.

What is Yalda Night?

The longest night of the year, on the last night of autumn, 30 Azar.
