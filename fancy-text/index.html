Fancy Text Generator

Type once, then copy your words in cool styles for Instagram, WhatsApp and more.

🔒 This tool runs on your device. Nothing you enter is sent or saved.

How to use it

  1. Type your name or words in the box.
  2. Every style updates as you type.
  3. Tap Copy next to the style you like and paste it into Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook or anywhere.

Common questions

How does it work?

These are not real fonts. They are special Unicode letters (like maths letters) that look different, so they still show when you paste them.

Why do some show as boxes?

A few older phones or apps do not have every special letter. Pick another style that shows well.

Is it safe for screen readers?

Screen readers may read fancy letters strangely, so use plain text for important information.
