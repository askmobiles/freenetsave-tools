How to use it

  1. Choose whether your grades are letters or percentages.
  2. For each course, pick or type the grade and its credits (use 1 if every course counts the same).
  3. Tap Add a course for more. Your GPA on the 4.0 scale shows at the bottom.

Common questions

Which scale does this use?

The common 4.0 scale used by many Canadian universities and the Ontario medical school application (OMSAS): A+ 90–100 = 4.0 down to F below 50 = 0. Open "See the grade scale used" for the full table.

My school uses a 4.3 or 12-point scale

Some schools do. Use this for a general 4.0 GPA, and check your school's own scale for the official number.

What are credits?

How much a course counts. A full-year university course is often 1.0 and a half course 0.5. If you are not sure, use 1 for every course.
