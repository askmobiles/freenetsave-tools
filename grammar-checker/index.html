🔒 This tool runs on your device. Your text is not sent or saved. Grammar checking uses the open-source Harper checker (Apache 2.0 licence).

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Choose your English: Canadian, American, British, Indian or Australian.
  3. Mistakes are underlined: red for grammar, orange for spelling, blue for style.
  4. Tap a suggestion to fix it, or tap Fix all. Tap Ignore if your text is already right.
  5. Tap Copy text and paste it where you need it.

Common questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The checker runs inside your browser on your own device. Your text is never uploaded or saved, so it is safe for school work, job applications and private messages.

Why does it take a moment the first time?

The checker downloads once (about 8 MB). After that your browser keeps it, so it opens quickly next time.

Does it check Canadian spelling?

Yes. Choose Canadian English to accept spellings like colour, centre and cheque. You can also choose American, British, Indian or Australian English.

Will it catch every mistake?

No checker is perfect. It finds common grammar, spelling and style mistakes, but always read your text again before you send it.

Does it work for other languages?

For now it checks English only.
