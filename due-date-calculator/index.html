Common questions

How is the due date worked out?

Pregnancy is counted as 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last period. Longer or shorter cycles move the date. From conception it is 266 days.

Will my baby come on this day?

Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the due date. Most come within two weeks before or after it.

My ultrasound gave a different date

Follow your doctor or midwife. An early ultrasound is usually more accurate than counting from a period.
