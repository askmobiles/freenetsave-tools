How to get the text out of a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. The words appear in the box. You can fix them there.
  3. Tap Copy all the text or save a text file.

A scanned PDF is a picture of a page, so it has no words to pull out. This tool tells you when that happens.

🔒 Your PDF is handled inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
