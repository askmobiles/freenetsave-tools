    Common questions

    What is a WebP file?

    WebP is a picture format made for websites. It is small, but some older programs, printers and upload forms do not accept it.

    JPG or PNG?

    Choose JPG for photos. Choose PNG if the picture has see-through parts (like a logo) that you want to keep see-through.

    Are my pictures uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.

    Can I convert other formats?

    Yes. This tool also opens PNG, GIF, BMP and AVIF pictures (where your browser supports them). The Picture converter can also make WebP files.
