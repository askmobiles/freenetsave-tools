Is it safe?

  • Your files stay with you. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else, and there is no sign-up.
  • It can't harm your device. It runs in the browser's safety box and can only see the one file you choose. At worst the device gets warm or slow for a moment.
  • No hacking risk from us. The program files come from this website and from jsDelivr (a large open-source code site), locked to exact versions. AI models come from Hugging Face, the main open-source AI library, locked to one exact version so they can't be changed.
  • Open source. We only use well-known AI models with open licences: ORMBG, MODNet, Whisper, Swin2SR, MediaPipe and Tesseract.

Common questions

Why does the first use take longer?

Your browser downloads the AI the first time. It is kept in your browser, so the next time starts fast. Use Wi-Fi if you are on a small data plan.

Are these as good as paid AI?

For these simple jobs they are usually good enough, and they are private. Always check the result, especially text from voice in languages other than English.

Do they work without internet?

After the first use, most of the work happens on your device, so a weak connection is fine.
