About this page

This page changes one English (Gregorian) date into about 20 calendars at once. For the full details of one calendar, such as the Tamil panchangam or the Chinese almanac, open its own page from the list below.

Dates are calculated on your device. Please check with your temple, mosque, synagogue, church or a local calendar for religious dates.

Questions

Which calendars does it cover?

Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Malayalam, Bengali, Indian national (Saka), Vikram Samvat, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Islamic (Hijri), Hebrew, Persian, Buddhist, Ethiopian, Coptic, Minguo and Julian.

Is anything saved?

No. The dates are worked out on your own device. Nothing you enter is sent or saved.

Can I print a calendar?

Yes. Choose a calendar in the month view and press Print this month. You get a one-page calendar with the English date and the other date in every box.
