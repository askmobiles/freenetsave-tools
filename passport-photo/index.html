Drag the photo so your head fits the oval: top of the head on the top line, chin on the bottom line.

Please check the official rules before you apply. Each country has rules on background, expression, glasses and how recent the photo is. Canada: paper passport applications need a photo taken by a commercial photographer, with the studio name, address and date on the back, so a home-made print is usually not accepted. Print the 4×6 sheet at "actual size" (100%), not "fit to page".
