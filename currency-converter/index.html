Common questions

Where do the rates come from?

From a free public exchange rate service, updated once a day. Only a request for today's rates is made — the amount you type is not sent anywhere.

Why does my bank give me less?

Banks, cards and money transfer shops add a fee or use a lower rate. Use this to see the real market rate and compare their offers.

Does it have the Sri Lankan rupee and Indian rupee?

Yes, both, plus over 150 other currencies.
