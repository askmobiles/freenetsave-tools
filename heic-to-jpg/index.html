    Common questions

    What is a HEIC file?

    HEIC (also called HEIF) is the photo format iPhones and iPads use by default. It keeps photos small, but many Windows computers, Android phones and websites cannot open it.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The conversion runs in your browser on your own device. Your photos never leave it.

    Does it keep the location of the photo?

    No. The new JPG does not include location, date or camera details, which is safer for sharing.

    How can I stop my iPhone taking HEIC photos?

    On the iPhone, open Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be JPG.

    Why is the first photo slow?

    On Windows and Android, the tool first loads a small HEIC reader (about 3 MB). After that, photos convert quickly.
