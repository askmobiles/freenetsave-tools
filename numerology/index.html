📖 How your numbers work

Birth number = the digits of your birth day added up (27 → 2+7 = 9). Destiny number = every digit of your full date of birth added up. Until age 30 the birth number is stronger; from 30 the destiny number leads. Lucky numbers, days and colours come from the planet that rules your birth number. A birth after 12 midnight counts as the next date.

📖 How the name check works

Every English letter has a value (see the chart). The name is checked two ways: the full total (e.g. 37) and the single digit it reduces to (3+7 = 10 → 1). Best is when the single digit is friendly to your numbers and the full total is a lucky one. If not, adding or changing one letter can move it to one of your best totals. Titles such as Mr, Mrs or Dr are not counted, and initials are shown on their own as well.

📖 How the business check works

A business name is matched to the owner’s numbers only. Totals of 1, 5 and 6 are traditionally strong for business. Use the same full name everywhere (sign, receipts, Google, phone greeting), because a shortened name has a different number.

📖 How the house & shop check works

House: it should suit the head of the family and not be bad for anyone else. Shop: only the owner counts. If the number doesn’t suit, a letter can be added on the door or nameplate (e.g. 23 → 23E). Your official address stays the same.

📖 How the vehicle check works

The plate’s digits and letters (using the letter chart) are added up. Enter the date of birth of the main driver or the owner. When choosing a new or personalised plate, aim for one of the strong totals shown.

📖 How the phone check works

All digits of the phone number are added up. Enter the date of birth of the person who uses the number. When getting a new number, pick one whose total is one of your strong totals shown.
