How to fill in a PDF form

  1. Choose the PDF form.
  2. Type your answers in the boxes and tick what applies. The boxes are listed in the order they are stored in the form.
  3. Tap Save filled PDF.

This works with PDFs that have real form boxes. If your PDF is a plain page or a scan, use the PDF editor to type on top of it instead.

🔒 Your PDF is handled inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
