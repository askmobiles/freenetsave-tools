How to crop a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Drag a box around the part of the page you want to keep.
  3. Tap Crop every page like this, or crop only the page you are looking at.

Cropping hides the edges; it does not shrink the words. The hidden part is still inside the file, so do not use cropping to hide private details. Use Cover up in the PDF editor for that.

🔒 Your PDF is handled inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
